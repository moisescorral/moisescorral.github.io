About the author

Moisés Corral Arce

He has built much of his professional career in sales and management environments, leading teams, customer acquisition processes and customer service, while actively taking part in training and mentoring sales-oriented professionals.

This book captures the spirit of hundreds of interviews, conversations and experiences with real salespeople: people who don't need tricks, but method, attitude and trust.
