06 / FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

Before
taking the step.

Everything you need to know to start your access application.

What exactly is CONTINUUM?+

CONTINUUM is the next step in human evolution: a revolutionary discovery that frees us from the limits of the physical body and opens a new era for our species.

Who can apply for residency?+

Any adult may apply for access to the system, provided their medical condition allows it. You'll find detailed information at any of the CONTINUUM regional offices located around the world. You may also include your loved ones in your application, so you never have to be separated from them.

Can I choose my city?+

Once inside the system and after completing the Transition process, you may move freely throughout CONTINUUM territory, except for technical areas and restricted-access communities.

How is the entry fee paid?+

The access fee must be paid in advance for each member included in the contract. If any person is unable to complete the Transition, the corresponding amount will be refunded to their legal heirs or previously designated beneficiaries.

What was left off the map

The sky belonged to everyone.
Until someone decided to manage it.

Campo la Carrera is a small, dry, stubborn place. While the world watches CONTINUUM's perfect cities in fascination, here a group of people keeps documents, memories and questions the Directorate would rather keep buried.

The arrival of an impossible message breaks the village's routine. Its source points to Thomas, the friendly official who smiles from the corporate website. But the voice in the message doesn't match the official version.
